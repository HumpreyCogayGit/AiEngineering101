# Harbor Bikes — Rentals

Harbor Bikes rents bicycles from two stations in Iloilo City: **Esplanade** and **Molo Plaza**.

## Prices

| Bike | Per hour | Per day (24 h) |
|---|---|---|
| City bike | ₱60 | ₱350 |
| E-bike | ₱150 | ₱900 |
| Tandem | ₱120 | ₱700 |
| Kids' bike (up to 12 years) | ₱40 | ₱250 |

A helmet and a lock are included with every rental. Child seats cost ₱50 per rental.

## Rules

- Riders must be **16 or older** to rent alone; younger riders need an adult on the same booking.
- E-bikes need a valid government ID and a ₱2,000 refundable deposit.
- The maximum rental is **7 days**. Longer rentals are handled by the corporate team.
- Bikes can be returned to either station.
