# Harbor Bikes — Policies

## Late returns

Late returns are charged per started hour at the hourly price, up to the price of one extra day.
After 24 hours late, the bike is reported missing and the deposit is kept.

## Damage

Small damage (flat tyres, a bent bell) is free. Other damage is charged at repair cost,
up to ₱5,000 for city bikes and ₱15,000 for e-bikes.

## Cancellations

Bookings cancelled at least **2 hours** before the start are refunded in full.
Later cancellations are refunded 50%. No-shows are not refunded.

## Weather

When PAGASA raises a tropical cyclone wind signal for Iloilo, all rentals are cancelled and refunded.
