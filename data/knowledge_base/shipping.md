# Shipping Information

## Where we ship

We ship to every region of the Philippines. International shipping is available
to Singapore, Hong Kong, Japan and Australia only.

## Delivery times

| Destination | Standard | Express |
|---|---|---|
| Metro Manila | 1–2 business days | Same day (order before 11:00) |
| Luzon (outside Metro Manila) | 3–5 business days | 2 business days |
| Visayas and Mindanao | 5–7 business days | 3 business days |
| International | 7–14 business days | Not available |

## Shipping fees

Standard shipping is **free for orders over ₱1,500**. Below that, standard
shipping costs ₱120 within Metro Manila and ₱180 elsewhere in the Philippines.
Express shipping costs ₱250. International shipping is a flat ₱950.

## Order tracking

You receive a tracking link by email and SMS as soon as your order ships.
Coffee is roasted to order, so orders ship 1 business day after roasting.
