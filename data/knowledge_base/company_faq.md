# Company FAQ

## About us

Nimbus Coffee Co. was founded in **2019** in Quezon City by two former baristas,
Ana Reyes and Miguel Santos. We roast Philippine-grown coffee in small batches
and buy directly from farmer cooperatives in Benguet, Sagada, Davao and Batangas.

## Café locations and opening hours

- **Makati** (Legazpi Village) — 6:30 to 21:00 daily
- **BGC** (High Street) — 7:00 to 22:00 daily
- **Quezon City** (Maginhawa) — 7:00 to 20:00, closed on Mondays

All cafés have free Wi-Fi and power outlets. Pets are welcome at the outdoor
tables of the BGC café.

## Wholesale

Restaurants and offices can order beans at wholesale prices (minimum 5 kg per
month). Email wholesale@nimbuscoffee.example.

## Sustainability

Our bags are compostable, and we pay farmers at least 30% above the fair-trade
minimum price. Bring your own cup to any café and get ₱20 off your drink.
