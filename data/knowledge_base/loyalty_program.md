# Nimbus Rewards (Loyalty Program)

Joining Nimbus Rewards is free in our mobile app.

- Earn **1 star for every ₱50** spent in our cafés or online.
- **100 stars** = one free drink of any size.
- **250 stars** = a free 250 g bag of beans.
- Members get a free pastry during their birthday month.

## Tiers

- **Green** (0–499 stars per year): standard rewards.
- **Gold** (500+ stars per year): earn stars 25% faster, free express shipping,
  and early access to seasonal drinks.

Stars expire **12 months** after they are earned. Stars cannot be exchanged
for cash or transferred to another member.
