# Refund and Return Policy

Nimbus Coffee Co. wants every customer to be happy with their purchase.

## Online orders

You can return unopened coffee beans and equipment within **30 days** of delivery
for a full refund. Opened coffee bags can be returned within **14 days** if you are
not satisfied with the taste; we refund 50% of the price as store credit.

To start a return, email returns@nimbuscoffee.example with your order number.
We send a prepaid return label within 2 business days. Refunds are issued to the
original payment method within **5–7 business days** after we receive the item.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, contact us within
**7 days** with a photo. We ship a replacement for free, and you do not need to
return the damaged item.

## In-store purchases

Drinks and pastries bought in our cafés cannot be returned, but if your drink is
not made correctly, ask the barista and we will remake it for free.

## Subscriptions

Subscription boxes can be cancelled at any time before the next billing date.
Boxes that have already shipped cannot be refunded, but can be paused.
