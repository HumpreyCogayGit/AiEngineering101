# Brewing Guide

## Pour-over

Use **15 g of coffee to 250 ml of water** (a 1:16 ratio). Grind medium-fine,
like table salt. Heat the water to 92–96 °C. Pour 40 ml first and wait 30
seconds — this is the *bloom*, which lets gas escape. Then pour the rest slowly
in circles. Total brew time should be about 3 minutes.

## French press

Use a 1:15 ratio with a coarse grind. Steep for 4 minutes, then press slowly.

## Cold brew

Use a 1:8 ratio with a very coarse grind and cold, filtered water. Steep in the
fridge for **16 to 18 hours**, then filter. Dilute 1:1 with water or milk. Cold
brew concentrate keeps for up to 10 days in the fridge.

## Espresso

Use 18 g of finely ground coffee for a 36 g double shot, extracted in 25–30
seconds. Barako Bold and Nimbus House Blend work best.

## Storage

Keep beans in an airtight container away from light and heat. Do not store
coffee in the fridge — moisture ruins the flavour. Beans taste best within
**4 weeks** of the roast date printed on the bag.
